# UBE Contributors

People, organizations and other entities who have helped UBE grow.  
  
UBE is an open-source project built on a simple principle: Everyone is welcome to contribute, in whatever way they can.  
Whether through code, testing, documentation, ideas, or funding AI development, every contribution is appreciated.  
  
This file recognizes those who have helped bring UBE closer to reality.  

## Recognition
Contributors may be credited under their real name, a pseudonym, or anonymously.  
  
Contributions are acknowledged regardless of their size. A bug report, a line of code, or a donation can all help move the project forward.
