Me

SpecifiedKeyword

Refers to the current class, form, or control instance.

Syntax

Me.member
member
A field, property or method on the current instance.

Details

Me is read-only and exists only while an instance member of a class, form or control is executing. It cannot be assigned or passed as writable direct ByRef storage. It is unavailable in standard modules, interfaces, declarations and initializers.
