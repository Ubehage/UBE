Until

SpecifiedLoop condition wordReserved wordUBE 1.0

Repeats or stops a Do loop according to when a condition becomes true.

Syntax

Do Until condition
    statements
Loop

Example

Sub Main()
    Dim Count As Long
    Do Until Count = 3
        Count = Count + 1
    Loop
    Print Count
End Sub

Expected result

3

Important rules

Until may appear after Do or after Loop, but not in both places for the same loop.
