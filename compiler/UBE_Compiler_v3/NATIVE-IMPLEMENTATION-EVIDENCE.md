## Resource accounting

`src/NativeResources.bas` implements scalar compiler-owned limits, live and
cumulative charges, high-water marks, controlled admission/release failures,
and post-write candidate-length admission. It has no graph, source, file or
worker policy. The initial limits and resource categories are those selected
in `NATIVE-RESOURCE-CONTRACT.md` and `NATIVE-BUILD-DIAGNOSTICS.md`.

Only live payload, pending entries and structural depth may be released.
Generated bytes, identities and work remain cumulative. A rejected charge
preserves the entire usage record. Admission checks subtraction before addition
so the accounting itself stays defined at the LongLong boundary. Candidate
length admission does not impose a disk quota on an external writer.
